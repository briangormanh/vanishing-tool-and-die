# Vanishing Tool & Die Makers

A data study on the decline of the U.S. tool & die workforce (2014-2024), built from BLS
Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics and Census County Business Patterns.

- **The study:** [index.html](https://briangormanh.github.io/vanishing-tool-and-die/)
- **Reviewer worksheet:** [audit/audit.html](https://briangormanh.github.io/vanishing-tool-and-die/audit/audit.html)

Both pages carry `noindex, nofollow`. This is a staging deploy for review, not a publication.
The study has NOT passed its accuracy review and must not be pitched until it has.

Two open issues at the time of this deploy:
1. The study uses BLS OEWS May 2024. BLS released May 2025 on 2026-05-15.
2. The Ohio headline reads -42.4% (BLS, occupation). Census (industry) shows -11.4% for the
   same period. Same direction, four times apart.
